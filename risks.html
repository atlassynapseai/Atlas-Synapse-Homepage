Risks we help you manage

These are the main risks across the AI lifecycle when there's no trust layer. Each section explains the risk and how Atlas Synapse helps.

← Home
01

Governance drift

Policies slip when models and data change. Without continuous checks, version drift goes unnoticed until it becomes a compliance finding or audit failure.

How Atlas Synapse helps

We validate policy versions at the input gate and flag drift so you can refresh before it impacts decisions. Full audit trail of which policy version applied to each request.

02

Policy conflict

Requests bypass or contradict guardrails. Unchecked inputs can trigger actions that violate internal or regulatory policy, exposing the organization to liability.

How Atlas Synapse helps

Input and output gates enforce policy before and after processing. Conflicting requests are blocked or routed to human review. Every block is logged with evidence.

03

Data leakage

Sensitive data enters prompts or outputs. PII, PHI, and confidential content can leak into logs, model responses, or downstream systems.

How Atlas Synapse helps

PII/PHI detection and masking at the input gate; redaction at the output gate. Configurable rules per domain. Evidence packages for compliance.

04

Fabricated outputs

Hallucinations and unsupported claims. Low-confidence or ungrounded outputs ship without verification, leading to wrong decisions and lost trust.

How Atlas Synapse helps

Output verification scores confidence and grounds. Low-confidence responses can be held for review or blocked. Audit trail includes scores and routing decisions.

05

Missing audit trails

No evidence for compliance or review. When regulators or internal audit ask how a decision was made, there's nothing to show.

How Atlas Synapse helps

Every request and response is tagged, traced, and exportable. Full evidence packages for regulators. Searchable audit log with filters by policy, risk type, and outcome.

06

Agent unpredictability

Autonomous steps deviate from intent. Multi-step agents can drift from approved workflows and produce unexpected or non-compliant outcomes.

How Atlas Synapse helps

We validate agent context at the boundary and log each step. Step-level bounds and routing rules keep agents within approved flows. Full trace for every run.
